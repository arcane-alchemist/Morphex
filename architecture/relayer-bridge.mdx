Morphex uses `RelayerVault` as a bridge between public ERC-20 stablecoins and confidential ERC-7984 tokens. You deposit public tokens into the vault, and an off-chain relayer mints confidential tokens to your wallet. To unwrap, you burn your confidential balance and request a public payout. If the relayer stalls, a time-locked escape hatch lets you claim your funds directly without trusting the relayer.

## Wrap flow

```text
Public ERC-20 → RelayerVault deposit → relayerMint → cToken (encrypted)
```

1. You call `RelayerVault.deposit(token, amount)` to move public ERC-20 into the vault.
2. The relayer detects the `Deposited` event, confirms the deposit in its internal ledger, and calls `MorphexToken.relayerMint(to, encryptedAmount, inputProof)`.
3. `relayerMint` is protected by `RELAYER_ROLE`. It lifts the encrypted amount with `FHE.fromExternal` and mints an encrypted balance to your wallet.

The minted amount is encrypted on-chain. Only you can decrypt your own confidential balance.

## Unwrap flow

```text
cToken → relayerBurnRequest → RelayerVault → batchWithdraw → public ERC-20
                             └→ escape hatch after delay (trustless fallback)
```

1. You call `MorphexToken.relayerBurnRequest(encryptedAmount, inputProof, publicAmount)`.
2. The contract burns your encrypted balance and calls `RelayerVault.registerWithdrawalRequest` to record your intent as a public tuple `(user, recipient, token, amount, requestedAt)`.
3. The relayer later batches payouts with `batchWithdraw`, paying public ERC-20 from the vault back to you.

If the relayer does not process your request in time, you can call `claimEscapedWithdrawal(requestId)` after `ESCAPE_HATCH_DELAY` has passed. This is a trustless fallback that pays you directly from the vault.

For payouts to a different address than the burner, use `relayerBurnRequestTo(recipient, ...)`, which routes through `RelayerVault.registerWithdrawalRequestTo`.

## Trust model

The relayer is trusted for liveness and honesty. It must:

- Mint confidential tokens promptly after public deposits.
- Batch and pay out withdrawals correctly.
- Maintain an internal ledger that reconciles vault balances against confidential supply.

`batchWithdraw` enforces that every payout matches the registered `(recipient, token, amount)` tuple. The relayer cannot redirect funds to a different address or token. If the relayer goes offline or misbehaves, the escape hatch gives users a direct, time-locked path to recover their public tokens.

## Relayer service specification

The in-repo specification at `docs/relayer-service-spec.md` defines the off-chain service requirements in detail, including:

- Ledger schema for deposits, mints, withdrawal requests, and batch payouts
- Netting and batching policy (hourly batches, minimum payout thresholds, recipient shuffling)
- Monitoring requirements and alert conditions
- Key management requirements (multisig or threshold-signing)
- Explicit non-goals (this is not a trustless or cryptographically unlinkable design)

See [Relayer Service Specification](/reference/relayer-service-spec) for the full text.

## Contract references

| Function | Contract | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `deposit(address token, uint256 amount)` | `RelayerVault` | Move public ERC-20 into the vault |
| `relayerMint(address to, externalEuint64 encryptedAmount, bytes inputProof)` | `MorphexToken` | Mint confidential tokens (relayer only) |
| `relayerBurnRequest(externalEuint64 encryptedAmount, bytes inputProof, uint256 publicAmount)` | `MorphexToken` | Burn cToken and register a withdrawal request |
| `relayerBurnRequestTo(address recipient, externalEuint64 encryptedAmount, bytes inputProof, uint256 publicAmount)` | `MorphexToken` | Same, with a different payout recipient |
| `batchWithdraw(Payout[] payouts, uint256[] requestIds)` | `RelayerVault` | Relayer pays out a batch of withdrawals |
| `claimEscapedWithdrawal(uint256 requestId)` | `RelayerVault` | User claims funds directly after the delay |

<Note>
  The relayer design is custodial and semi-trusted. A production deployment should use a multisig-controlled relayer signer, hourly batching with reconciliation, and monitoring for vault solvency and pending escape-hatch deadlines.
</Note>
